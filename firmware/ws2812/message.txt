CLAUDE- vdisk: base64 web tool (106 KB), LOG.TXT; hidout: log and monitor events

- WEBTOOL.HTM: scripts without indentation, gzip stored as base64
  (decoded by the browser) instead of hex: 166 KB -> 106 KB.
- vdisk: LOG.TXT on the drive (last 4 KB, vdisk_log()), remount after
  new lines.
- hidout: logs table, selector, interfaces and per-line sends to
  LOG.TXT (weak link to vdisk_log), and reports 0xFFFE0xnn monitor
  events, for debugging the K400r Fn lock.
