CLAUDE- K400r Fn lock: feature index 0F, resend every 5 s

The K400 Plus bytes (index 09) did nothing on the K400r. Its
FN_INVERSION feature is at index 0F (found and confirmed on the
keyboard): 10 01 0F 1A 00|01 00 00. The examples now send that, and
write 0 to register 1 for one tick every 5 s so hidout resends it,
because the keyboard forgets the setting when switched off.
