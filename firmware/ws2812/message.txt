CLAUDE- vdisk/hidout: stop remounting the drive every few seconds

- hidout: log a device reply only when it differs from the last one
  (the K400 example resends every 5 s and gets the same answer).
- vdisk: remount for new LOG.TXT lines at most every 30 s.
