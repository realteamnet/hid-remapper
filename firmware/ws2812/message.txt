CLAUDE- hidout: message table; K400 family Fn lock example; RGB example LED-only

- hidout reads its messages from a table in an expression (default 8):
  one line per message, <length> <report words> <targets>, targets
  0xVVVV (any product of a vendor) or 0xVVVVPPPP, none = any device.
  A selector register (default 1) has a bit per line. Lines are sent
  when selected, again when a matching device reappears, when the table
  changes, or after the selector was 0 for a tick. Up to 64-byte reports.
- hidout_example.json: Logitech K400/K400r (index 0F, confirmed on a
  K400r) and K400 Plus (index 09) Fn lock on Fn+Esc, with a model
  setting, the Fn lock at boot, and a resend every 5 s.
- ws2812_example.json: the K400 parts are removed; LED only again.
