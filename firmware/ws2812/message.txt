CLAUDE- hidout: log the device's reply after a send

hidout_report_received() is called from report_received_callback()
(gated). For 2 s after hidout sends a report to a device plugged into
the remapper, up to 8 input reports from that interface go to LOG.TXT,
e.g. the HID++ confirmation or error from a Logitech receiver.
