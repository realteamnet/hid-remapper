CLAUDE- ws2812_led_show: optional lit-time and flash-delay params

ws2812_led_show(lit_ms = WS2812_INFINITE, flash_ms = 0):
- lit_ms: how long to keep the LED lit (ms). WS2812_INFINITE (default)
  shows and returns immediately (non-blocking, unchanged behaviour).
- flash_ms: when > 0, blink on/off every flash_ms until lit_ms elapses;
  0 (default) = no blink. A finite lit_ms blocks for that duration then
  turns the LED off.

Existing no-arg callers (init/activity) keep their current behaviour via
the defaults.
