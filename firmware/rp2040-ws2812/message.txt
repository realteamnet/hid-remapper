CLAUDE- ws2812: register numbers as CMake settings (default 31/32)

WS2812_SETTINGS_REGISTER (31) and WS2812_COLOR_REGISTER (32) are now CMake
variables passed to the remapper target, so a project that needs those
registers can move them (e.g. -DWS2812_COLOR_REGISTER=8). Defaults unchanged.
