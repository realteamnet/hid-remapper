CLAUDE- ws2812: no built-in colours; LED shows only the config's register 32

Remove the power-on sweep and the green activity flash (and
ws2812_led_activity_on() plus its two main.cc call sites). The LED is off
at init and shows register 32 (0xBBRRGGBB) as set by the config's
expressions, e.g. per-layer colours via layer_state. Nothing about colours
or layers is hardcoded in the firmware.
