CLAUDE- ws2812 PoC: PIO driver, standalone test firmware, gated integration

Re-implement WS2812 support for the Waveshare RP2040-Zero (GPIO16) in
firmware/rp2040-ws2812:
- ws2812.pio: line-by-line conversion of the MicroPython rp2 asm sample
- ws2812_led.cc/.h: PIO driver (free SM auto-claimed), power-on R/G/B
  sweep, green activity blink, WS2812_RGB_ORDER switch
- ws2812_test.cc: standalone ws2812_test.uf2 (R,G,B,W cycle, no USB) to
  prove the driver independently of hid-remapper
- ws2812.cmake: all build logic

Upstream edits: one include(... OPTIONAL) line in CMakeLists.txt and
#ifdef WS2812_ENABLED blocks in main.cc. Stock activity_led calls kept.
